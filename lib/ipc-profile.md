## Overview

IntegrityPro Consulting (IPC) is a federal and commercial AI/ServiceNow consultancy that helps organizations modernize through agentic AI, ServiceNow platform engineering, and enterprise process automation.

## Core Services

- **AI / Agentic Systems** — design, build, and deploy LLM-based agents and copilots; RAG architectures; agent orchestration; evaluation and observability.
- **ServiceNow Implementation** — platform engineering, Now Assist enablement, custom application development, integrations, ITSM/ITOM/CSM/HRSD.
- **Process Automation** — workflow design, integration platforms, RPA where appropriate.
